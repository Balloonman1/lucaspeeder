How to ride

W / SThrottle, brake, reverse A / DSteer SPACEJump (-2 energy). Double-tap for a backflip (-5) FThrow tomato

On a phone: turn it sideways. The bike drives itself. Drag your left thumb to steer. Tap the right side to jump, double-tap for a backflip, hold it to brake. Tomato button is on the right edge.

Energy drains every second. Follow the GPS to food. Energy drinks double your speed. Cherries clone you with a helper rider. A jump throws you forward but costs 2 energy; a double-tap backflip goes higher and further for 5. Tomatoes aren't for eating: throw one at a rival and their GPS goes dark for 10 seconds. Run out of energy and you're out. Last one alive wins. Races last 5 minutes at most; if the clock runs out, the rider with the most energy wins.
